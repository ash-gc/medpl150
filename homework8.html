This story follows the pigeon on its daily search for food. The pigeon, one of many in the city, starts at home on Hunter College campus. The city provides many options to eat. At the same time, many challenges and the competition for food require decisive thinking and a bit of luck. The pigeon will meet fellow locals (animals and humans) and tourists. The players will navigate through the city and help the pigeon feed itself before the end of the night.
